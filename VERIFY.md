## 1. Reserve Foundation & Key Wildlife Facts
- **Establishment:** Established in 1936 as Hailey National Park (India's first national park), later renamed Ramganga National Park in 1954–55, and renamed Jim Corbett National Park in 1956.
- **Project Tiger Launch:** 1973; Project Tiger was inaugurated at Dhikala Forest Rest House inside Corbett Tiger Reserve.
- **Total Area:** 1,288.31 sq. km (Core Critical Tiger Habitat: 821.99 sq. km; Buffer / Peripheral Area: 466.32 sq. km).
- **Tiger Population:** All India Tiger Estimation (AITE) 2022 recorded 260 tigers within the Corbett Tiger Reserve landscape — the highest tiger density and population of any tiger reserve in India.
- **Fauna:** Bengal tiger (*Panthera tigris*), Indian leopard, Asian elephant (*Elephas maximus*), spotted deer (chital), sambar, hog deer (*Axis porcinus* — Dhikala grassland is the primary stronghold in CTR), barking deer (kakar), gharial (*Gavialis gangeticus*) and mugger crocodile (*Crocodylus palustris*) in the Ramganga reservoir/river, smooth-coated otters, sloth bear, yellow-throated marten.
- **Avifauna:** Over 600 recorded bird species (UTDB cites 650+ resident and migratory species including Great Hornbill, Pallas's Fish Eagle, Tawny Fish Owl, Ibisbill, Crested Serpent Eagle, Plum-headed Parakeet).

## 2. Tourism Zones Inside Corbett Tiger Reserve (CTR)

| Zone Name | Entry Gate | Official Season | Administrative Base | Key Characteristics & Wildlife Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dhikala** | Dhangarhi Gate | 15 Nov – 15 Jun | Ramnagar (32 km to Dhangarhi) | Largest, most iconic core zone. Expansive Dhikala Chaur grasslands, Ramganga river valley, Patli Dun valley. Historic 1890 British-era Forest Rest House. **Strict rule:** NO day jeep permits permitted; accessible exclusively via Canter Safari or Forest Rest House overnight stay permit. Strongest area for elephant herds, hog deer, and tigers. |
| **Bijrani** | Amdanda Gate | 15 Oct – 30 Jun | Ramnagar (1 km to Amdanda Gate) | Dense sal forest transitioning into grasslands and riverine tracts. Historically renowned for high tiger sighting frequency and diverse birdlife. Day jeep safaris operate morning and afternoon. |
| **Jhirna** | Dhela Gate | Year-Round *(subject to weather & track conditions)* | Ramnagar (approx. 20 km to Dhela Gate) | Southern boundary of CTR. Dry deciduous forest with bamboo brakes and open scrub. Open during monsoon months when river crossings permit. Frequented by wild elephants, sloth bears, and deer. |
| **Dhela** | Dhela Gate | Year-Round *(subject to weather & track conditions)* | Ramnagar (approx. 20 km to Dhela Gate) | Eco-tourism zone opened in 2014, sharing the Dhela gate with Jhirna. Mixed forest, waterholes, and watchtowers; good birding and ungulate sightings. |
| **Durga Devi** | Durgadevi Gate | 15 Nov – 15 Jun | Ramnagar (approx. 28–30 km on Ranikhet road) | Rugged, hilly terrain bordering the Ramganga and Mandal rivers. Renowned for birdwatching (crested kingfishers, forktails, fish eagles) and mahseer in the rivers. High elevation landscape. |
| **Garjiya** | Garjiya Gate *(Ringora)* | 15 Oct – 30 Jun *(Monsoon closure 1 Jul – 14 Oct)* | Ramnagar (approx. 9–10 km) | **Conflict Resolved:** Official pricing and operational schedules confirm season operates from 15 October through 30 June. Beautiful forest bordering the Kosi river basin; popular tiger and leopard territory. |
| **Sonanadi** | Vatanvasa Gate | 15 Nov – 15 Jun | Kotdwar / Kalagarh | Part of the Sonanadi Wildlife Sanctuary buffer. Serene, less crowded forest rich in elephants, leopards, and birds. Ideal for wilderness purists. |
| **Pakhro** | Pakhro Gate | 15 Nov – 15 Jun | Kotdwar (approx. 25 km from Kotdwar railway station; ~2 hrs from Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun) | Convenient entry point from western Uttarakhand/Dehradun. Pure sal and mixed forest; elephant and tiger movements connecting Rajaji and Corbett corridors. |

## 3. Buffer & Adjacent Forest Divisions (Outside CTR)
*Must be distinctly and honestly marked as **Outside Corbett Tiger Reserve**:*
1. **Sitabani (Ramnagar Forest Division):** Beautiful landscape associated with Valmiki Ashram legends. Does not fall under CTR jurisdiction. Offers open jeep safaris and walking trails permitted by Ramnagar FD.
2. **Kota Zone (Ramnagar Forest Division):** Adjacent buffer forest tracts bordering Sitabani and the Kosi river.
3. **Phato Zone (Terai West Forest Division):** Tree house stays and jeep safaris administered by Terai West Forest Division; renowned for peaceful sal forests and waterhole hides.

## 4. Official CTR Fee Schedule (Verified 2026-27 Rates)
*Official fees last verified: 06 November 2026. Corbett Tiger Reserve administration may revise rates.*

### A. Day Jeep Safari (Per Permit / Up to 6 Travellers + max 2 children under 12)
- **Government Permit Fee (Indian Nationals / OCI / SAARC):** ₹3,380 per jeep
- **Government Permit Fee (Foreign Nationals):** ₹6,680 per jeep
- **Registered Gypsy / Vehicle Fee (Paid to registered vehicle operator):**
  - Standard Zones (Dhela, Jhirna, Garjiya, Durga Devi): ₹3,000
  - Bijrani Zone: ₹2,700
  - Pakhro Zone: ₹2,800
  - Sonanadi Zone: ₹2,800 – ₹4,500
- **Mandatory Authorised Nature Guide Fee:** ₹1,000 per safari shift
- **Concessions (Indian Nationals on valid ID):**
  - Senior Citizens (Age 60+): ₹250 concession per eligible traveller on permit
  - Students (Age 12–18 with valid institution ID): ₹375 concession per eligible student
- **Foreign National Rule:** If any traveller in the jeep booking is a foreign national, the entire permit is charged at the foreign national rate.

### B. Canter Safari (Dhikala Zone Exclusively)
- **Vehicle Type:** 16-seater heavy-duty open safari canter operated directly under CTR oversight.
- **Boarding Points:** Ramnagar Reception Office and Dhangarhi Gate.
- **Indian Nationals:** ₹1,500 + 18% GST per seat.
- **Foreign Nationals:** ₹3,000 + 18% GST per seat.
- **Booking Limit:** Maximum 4 seats per individual transaction.
- **Punctuality Warning:** The canter operates strictly on government gate schedules and does not wait for late passengers under any circumstances.

### C. Forest Rest House (FRH) Overnight Night Stay
- **Accommodation Units:** Dhikala FRH, Sarpduli FRH, Gairal FRH, Sultan FRH, Bijrani FRH, Malani FRH, Jhirna FRH, Dhela FRH, Pakhro FRH, Sonanadi FRH.
- **Room Tariff (1 Room, 2 Persons, 1 Night, includes Zone Entry Permit):**
  - Indian Nationals: ₹3,500 – ₹5,050 per room/night
  - Foreign Nationals: ₹10,450 per room/night
- **Mandatory Registered Safari Gypsy + Driver inside FRH:** ₹5,750 – ₹7,750 (e.g. Dhikala FRH: ₹7,250; Sarpduli: ₹7,750) covering transit and in-zone game drives.
- **Mandatory Guide Fee:** ₹1,000 per safari session / day.
- **Strict FRH Living Guidelines:**
  - Absolute prohibition of non-vegetarian food, alcohol, firecrackers, and loud sound.
  - Zero night vehicle movement inside the reserve between sunset and sunrise.
  - Several remote rest houses (Malani, Sultan, Kanda) require guests to carry dry provisions and cooking rations for the resident forest cook. Solar electricity is restricted to limited evening hours.

## 5. Official CTR Rules, Mandates & Legal Compliance (2026)
1. **Supreme Court Mobile Phone Ban:** In compliance with Supreme Court environmental guidelines, mobile phones are strictly prohibited during safaris inside CTR core and tourism zones (effective 5 February 2026). Phones must remain deposited at accommodation/gate lockers. Dedicated cameras (DSLR, mirrorless, compact) and binoculars are permitted and encouraged.
2. **Mandatory Photo Identity Verification:** From 22 September 2026, the primary booking leader must upload clear front and back scans of government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID). All other group members' ID numbers must be recorded. Original physical ID cards must be presented at the entry gate. Any typographical mismatch or missing ID results in immediate forfeiture and denied entry.
3. **Advance Booking Calendar:**
   - Indian Citizens: Rolling booking opens **45 days in advance** at 10:00 AM IST.
   - Foreign Nationals: Rolling booking opens **90 days in advance**.
4. **Same-Day Online Cut-Off Times:**
   - Morning Safari Slot: Booking closes strictly at **06:30 AM**.
   - Afternoon Safari Slot: Booking closes strictly at **04:00 PM**.
5. **Permit Non-Transferability:** Permits issued by CTR are 100% non-refundable, non-transferable, and cannot be rescheduled or modified once generated.
6. **Zero Plastic & Environmental Sanctions:** Corbett Tiger Reserve is a strict zero-single-use-plastic zone. Littering, honking, stepping out of vehicles inside the forest, and throwing trash carries a ₹500 spot penalty and potential prosecution under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972.

## 6. Transit & Access Distances
- **Railhead:** Ramnagar Railway Station (RMR) is directly connected to Old Delhi (Ranikhet Express, Corbett Park Link Express).
- **Road Distance from Delhi:** 245 km – 260 km via NH9 (Delhi → Hapur bypass → Moradabad bypass → Kashipur → Ramnagar); typical drive time 5.5 to 6.5 hours.
- **Nearest Commercial Airport:** Pantnagar Airport (PGH) is approx. 80–85 km from Ramnagar (approx. 2 hours via NH309). Dehradun Jolly Grant Airport (DED) is convenient for Kotdwar/Pakhro gates (approx. 110 km / 2.5 hours).
